## Claims checked against evidence
- "gain comes from the calibrated operating point, not switching": validation C0 36.08 vs C3 36.11; test-dev anchor 33.93 vs Q 33.84, Δ −0.09 [−0.25, +0.06].
- "Q is not better on every metric": 123 more identity switches than the hand-designed controller, significant.
- "real time": processing throughput of decoded frames on a T4 ≥ 25 FPS; with decoding 21.90 FPS (stated).
- "switching does not measurably beat a well-calibrated static point" is stated only for the primary study and the U2MOT held-out experiment; the SparseTrack row is in-sample, gains over the declared 0.75 comparator (+0.13 [+0.02, +0.24]) and not over the default 0.70 (−0.07 [−0.54, +0.19]).
- No claim that one controller transferred across pipelines; no throughput comparison for the cross-pipeline rows.
- No embedded-board, flight, or official-protocol claim is made.
