\section{Limitations}
The modern seven-sequence VisDrone result is a second validation read: a static
control had already read those sequences before ours. It is therefore a frozen,
documented comparison, not an untouched held-out confirmation; the historical
test-dev experiment is the cleaner historical held-out evidence.

The freeze records that Trial 7 is not the literal argmax of the documented
selection rule under either archived eligibility interpretation. This provenance
limitation is reported without rerunning the search. Matched-static and
shuffled-timing controls further weaken a claim that exact switching timing
causes the accuracy gain. The project class-agnostic evaluator is not an
official VisDrone leaderboard protocol.

Only one modern detector checkpoint is used: a third-party
VisDrone-DET-trained YOLO11m model rather than a VisDrone-MOT-trained or
published OATrack detector. Two tracker hosts demonstrate interaction but not
tracker independence, and the OATrack implementation is a controlled
repository reimplementation rather than a direct published-system comparison.

UAVDT transfer is a single frozen-policy run per host without cross-validation
or bootstrap. Its ByteTrack result is strongly positive in MOTA and FP, while
OATrack HOTA is essentially flat. T4 timing is measured, but image-read work is
not instrumented identically in baseline and our harnesses. Finally, throughput
is below 30 FPS. These limits constrain generalization and attribution while
leaving a reproducible detector-operating-point study with measured quality,
cost, and transfer behaviour.
