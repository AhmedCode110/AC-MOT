\section{AC--MOT V2: Identity-Aware Multi-Objective Optimization}
V1 established that validation-derived detector operating points could improve
the historical quality profile, but quality was not the only deployment goal.
Identity fragmentation and compute can move in a different direction from
MOTA. V2 therefore retained the frozen V1 search design while changing the
selection question from a constrained quality profile to an explicit
identity--runtime trade-off.

The V2 validation study maximized MOTA and minimized IDS subject only to
$\mathrm{FPS}\geq25$; it removed the V1 $\mathrm{IDS}\leq271$ gate. It used
Optuna's seeded TPE sampler, validation data only, and the same five-cue SCI,
supported detector operating points, seven-entry temporal window, and
ten-frame analysis stride. The implementation explicitly constructed a
feasible Pareto front: a trial dominated another when it had no lower MOTA and
no higher IDS, with at least one strict improvement. The archive records
highest-MOTA, lowest-IDS, and balanced Pareto candidates. The balanced choice
used the documented equal-weight normalized MOTA/IDS score, with HOTA, IDF1,
FPS, and trial index as tie-breaks; Pareto terminology is therefore an
implementation fact rather than a retrospective label \cite{deb2002nsga2}.

Trial22 was the official selected V2 configuration. Its validation record was
MOTA 19.330, HOTA 31.651, IDF1 34.226, IDS 168, and 51.406 FPS. The frozen
profile used weights $w_c=0.1646453$, $w_t=0.1746265$, and $w_e=0.5076113$;
$w_n=0.1206956$ and $w_b=0.0324213$. Its confidence endpoints were
$0.40/0.40$, NMS endpoints $0.60/0.35$, and SCI thresholds
$0.2927136/0.6661672$, with the same $512/928/960$ operating levels. The
historical post-selection technical testdev rerun gave MOTA 23.792, HOTA
31.218, IDF1 37.870, IDS 919, and 46.024 FPS; these are reported as a
secondary post-hoc record, not as the validation values that selected Trial22.

The V1/V2 distinction is consequently objective-dependent: V1 is the
quality-oriented profile, while V2 is the identity/runtime profile. Neither
profile replaces the other for every tracker or metric, and V1's IDS must not
be described as worse than the baseline (1184 versus 1235 in the archived
held-out record). The complete parameter records, all historical trials, and
the uncertainty tables are retained in Online Resource~1.
